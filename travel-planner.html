Travel Tips for Sri Lanka

Best time to visit: December to March for the south and west coasts; April to September for the east coast. The hill country is pleasant year-round.

Visa: Most nationalities require an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA), obtainable online before arrival. Apply at eta.gov.lk.

Getting around: The train network is scenic and affordable. Tuk-tuks are ideal for short distances. Private drivers are recommended for multi-day tours.

Currency: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). ATMs are widely available in cities and tourist areas. Credit cards accepted at most hotels and restaurants.

Safety: Sri Lanka is considered very safe for tourists. Petty theft can occur in crowded areas — use common sense and keep valuables secured.
