About Sri Lanka

An Island of Infinite Wonder

Nestled in the warm waters of the Indian Ocean just south of India, Sri Lanka is a teardrop-shaped island that packs an extraordinary diversity of experiences into its 65,610 square kilometres. Eight UNESCO World Heritage Sites, over 1,000 kilometres of pristine coastline, misty mountains, ancient rainforests, and some of the world's finest tea estates await the curious traveller.

Known as the Pearl of the Indian Ocean, Sri Lanka has captivated explorers, traders, and travellers for millennia. Arab merchants, Portuguese sailors, Dutch colonists, and British planters all left their mark — creating a fascinating cultural tapestry that is uniquely Sri Lankan. Today, this warm, hospitable island offers world-class wildlife safaris, culinary adventures, spiritual journeys, and beach escapes, all within easy reach of each other.

Whether you seek the thrill of climbing a 5th-century rock fortress at sunrise, the meditative calm of a Buddhist pilgrimage route, the satisfaction of a perfect surf wave, or simply the pleasure of a freshly brewed cup of Ceylon tea overlooking emerald plantations — Sri Lanka will exceed your every expectation.
